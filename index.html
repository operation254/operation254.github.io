Executive Biography

Kevin Kariuki Njagi is a Nairobi-based Business Development and Operations professional with experience leading growth, sales, and operational execution across healthcare, pharmaceutical, technology, and service-based organizations. He has supported business setup, sales pipeline development, patient acquisition, supplier partnerships, workforce operations, CRM management, reporting, and commercial strategy.

Kevin currently works across healthcare and pharmaceutical growth through Tokuyoshi Pharmaceuticals Limited and Kings & Queens Medics. His role combines strategic planning with hands-on execution: supplier negotiation, commercial setup, pricing support, sales team coordination, lead generation, pipeline control, patient conversion, and revenue visibility.

Earlier in his career, Kevin founded InfoTeck Technologies, where he built client pipelines, managed consultations, prepared proposals, coordinated delivery, and maintained long-term client relationships. His operations experience at Lifetouch and Shutterfly added workforce analytics, escalation handling, performance reporting, and resource coordination across teams of 30 to 180 agents.
