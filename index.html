Executive Profile

Executive Biography

Kevin Kariuki Njagi is a Nairobi-based Business Development and Operations professional with experience leading growth, sales, and operational execution across healthcare, pharmaceutical, consumer packaged goods (CPG), technology, and service-based organizations. He has supported business setup, sales pipeline development, patient acquisition, supplier partnerships, workforce operations, CRM management, reporting, and commercial strategy.

Kevin currently works across healthcare and consumer goods through Tokuyoshi Pharmaceuticals Limited and Kings & Queens Medics. In the CPG and skincare sector, Kevin manages a diverse portfolio of mass-market and dermatological brands—including CeraVe, La Roche-Posay, Nivea, Garnier, L'Oréal, The Ordinary, Clinique, and Neutrogena. He oversees inventory availability, retail/e-commerce channel expansion, and aligns digital marketing campaigns with physical product launches.

Earlier in his career, Kevin founded InfoTeck Technologies, where he built DTC client pipelines, managed Shopify storefronts, and drove paid media campaigns. His operations and analytical experience at Lifetouch and Shutterfly added workforce analytics, escalation handling, performance reporting, and resource coordination across teams of 30 to 180 agents. He also brings deep experience in financial administration (AP/AR, QuickBooks, SAP) and talent acquisition (recruiting for C-level, healthcare, and workforce roles).
